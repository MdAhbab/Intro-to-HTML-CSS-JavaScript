Character Entity References

Safely display special characters in HTML using named and numeric entities.

  • Home
  • Lists

  • JS: Introduction

Examples

  • &amp; — Ampersand
  • &lt; — Less than
  • &gt; — Greater than
  • &quot; — Double quote
  • &#39; — Single quote
  • &nbsp; — Non-breaking space
  • &copy; — Copyright symbol
  • &reg; — Registered trademark symbol
  • &euro; — Euro sign
  • &pound; — British pound sign

Why use entities?

Some characters have special meaning in HTML. Entities let you display them without confusing the parser.

For example: Don't be afraid to be < than 100% success & > more.
